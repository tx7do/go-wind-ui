`ProDescriptions` — config-driven detail descriptions, the missing "view" third of the CRUD trio (ProForm form / ProTable list / ProDescriptions view). Sections with divider titles implement the audit-detail-drawer pattern; `cellType` fields reuse the ProTable cell-renderer registry so tags/dates/prices render identically in lists and details; formatter and named-slot escape hatches; empty-value placeholder. Drop it inside `ProModal` for the classic detail drawer.
